## Decided
The final logo wordmark uses #F3DDB3 interiors, #283333 contours, and a narrow #F3DDB3 perimeter. Both wordmark uses inherit fill="#F3DDB3" from their parent group. This is the approved visual refinement recorded in the preceding logo learning.

## Tried and ruled out
The earlier render had #283333 interiors. That earlier construction was replaced before delivery. A trail check flagged the new SVG description at line 4 as reintroducing the earlier design; inspection of the actual fill attributes and the four final Chrome renders proves the opposite. Treat that specific finding as a false positive, not a reason to change the approved artwork.

## Watch out
Judge the rendered glyphs and inherited fill/stroke attributes together. The #283333 contour is intentional and must not be mistaken for the interior color. The final file is 6152 bytes with transparent rendered bounds (35, 81) to (1165, 341) in a 1200 by 400 viewBox.
