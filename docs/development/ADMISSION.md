# Original implementation admission

This document introduces the project's material and use admission records and indexes their detailed evidence. The objective remains the full native game, not a collection of bounded experiments. See the [project introduction](../../README.md), [current status and qualification boundaries](../STATUS.md), and [licensing and provenance policy](../planning/LICENSING.md).

## Admission and evidence boundaries

Admission is a candidate-scoped decision about identified material, its permitted use, ownership, and containment. It is not execution evidence, blanket source relicensing, a completed dependency audit, or release approval. Public synthetic fixtures establish only the mechanisms they actually exercise; private-input records retain their own source and use restrictions.

The root MIT grant applies to original authored portions only where contributors own the rights. Third-party implementations, adapted game bodies, private ROM inputs, and their assets retain their separately documented rights and provenance boundaries. Admission does not place those materials under the project's MIT grant or authorize their redistribution.

Every execution record is limited to its immutable source and executable revisions, dependency and input identities, configuration, and exercised cohort. Historical proofs do not qualify later executables. Earlier observations remain historical even when later work uses the same component names or routes.

Neither material admission nor an individual mechanism or campaign record establishes blanket release readiness, gameplay completeness, fidelity, or Windows 11 qualification. Linux evidence does not qualify Windows 11. Read historical campaign results separately from the current installed-product evidence and the limits in [STATUS](../STATUS.md).

The [installed runtime records](admission/INSTALLED_RUNTIME.md) cover Linux first-course and cold-continuation evidence. They are deliberately discoverable here as bounded installed-product records, not as proof of a full successful campaign. The [sealed controller records](admission/SEALED_CONTROLLER.md) describe the associated durable-progress and identity boundaries.
