# Independent paired assessment and adjudication

## Method and authority

Three targets received the same question in separate planner and reviewer first passes: what should be retained, amended or deferred in the existing planning contracts, on pinned source evidence, with a counterexample, strongest alternative/tradeoff and falsifying experiment? GatePlanner/GateReviewer assessed dependencies and admission; PhotoPlanner/PhotoReviewer assessed photography/rendering; RuntimePlanner/RuntimeReviewer assessed timing, continuations, audio and ownership. All six complete reports informed the parent decision. Their proposals are attributed below; these are not implementation-review findings.

The parent adjudicated evidence and risks, **not a majority vote**. Agreement does not prove feasibility; a minority challenge can expose an unmet proof obligation. H1–H8 are the resulting shared contracts. The reviews and this integration are planning-only: no runtime, reference extraction, platform, permission or renderer/audio qualification was performed. [VERIFICATION](VERIFICATION.md) remains the historical record, unchanged and not relabeled as this assessment's proof. Beads alone owns live issue status; this record is neither a task checklist nor a completion ledger.

[BASELINES](BASELINES.md) owns all pins. Citations below use Snap `df8815e75f3d79618466e01e0827c6165b93d11c` and LUS `759b13401cd6903daa67a00a753066f791622e78`. Source facts are distinguished from **[INFERENCE]** about candidate failures. [PLAN](PLAN.md#decision-register) integrates decisions, [ROADMAP](ROADMAP.md) owns admission/whole-unit dependencies, [VALIDATION](VALIDATION.md) owns future proof, [DECOMP](DECOMP.md) owns game semantics, and [LUS / Ghostship](LUS_GHOSTSHIP.md) owns host API limits. Those owner contracts replace, rather than duplicate, the full role reports.

## Photography target: H2–H4

**Planner proposal.** PhotoPlanner RDP-01/ORACLE-02/PASS-03/FRAME-04/COMPARE-05 separated rasterization from export, required transient acquisition, froze reconstructed transactions, separated presentation from feedback and proposed architecture-independent exact comparators. A canonical semantic rasterizer was the strongest alternative, not an already proved necessity.

**Reviewer challenge.** PhotoReviewer R1–R5 emphasized that address-only target dispatch plus readback cannot establish independent depth surfaces; post-quantization cannot repair prior visibility decisions. Live color needs original coverage/history too, so evaluation-only software is insufficient. Hidden RDP-state requirements remain research questions. Exact packed-value expectations conflict with acceptance conditional on what a chosen renderer promises.

**Parent choice — amend H2/H4, retain neutral H3.** Freeze both feedback domains and default exact live words/raw packed-depth words, per-pixel predicates/counts/decisions. Observational equivalence needs a separately reviewed revision proving subsequent raster/copy/predicate behavior, not equal totals. Capture completed live tiles before shared-bank reuse and both completed scoring passes before clear/reconstruction, with object order, generation, transforms/raster state and perturbation checks. Complete wide/normal reconstructed passes separately; retain their operands until completion. Mandatory feedback cannot disappear with presentation readiness. GPU/software/compute/hybrid rendering, decoder translation versus interpretation, transfer policy and backend qualification remain deferred.

**Rejected alternative/tradeoff.** A readback-only fix or RGBA/depth packing shader is smaller but cannot repair discarded fragments or ignored target changes **[INFERENCE]**. A full software/RDP implementation might make state explicit but adds unmeasured semantic/performance burden; it is not mandated. Reusing one mutable world or deriving a pass by crop is unproved. Presentation tolerances and path-dependent raw-byte promises are rejected because they can hide a discrete decision change.

**Source evidence.** [Target clears/copies and raster modes](https://github.com/ethteck/pokemonsnap/blob/df8815e75f3d79618466e01e0827c6165b93d11c/src/app_render/score.c#L53-L132), [both subject draws](https://github.com/ethteck/pokemonsnap/blob/df8815e75f3d79618466e01e0827c6165b93d11c/src/app_render/score.c#L169-L213), [pass reuse/counts](https://github.com/ethteck/pokemonsnap/blob/df8815e75f3d79618466e01e0827c6165b93d11c/src/app_render/score.c#L230-L321), [camera constants](https://github.com/ethteck/pokemonsnap/blob/df8815e75f3d79618466e01e0827c6165b93d11c/src/app_render/47380.c#L1116-L1133) and [reconstruction](https://github.com/ethteck/pokemonsnap/blob/df8815e75f3d79618466e01e0827c6165b93d11c/src/app_render/47380.c#L1654-L1680) establish the pass contract. [Live copies/comparison](https://github.com/ethteck/pokemonsnap/blob/df8815e75f3d79618466e01e0827c6165b93d11c/src/app_level/pokemon_detect.c#L193-L239) and [DP publication](https://github.com/ethteck/pokemonsnap/blob/df8815e75f3d79618466e01e0827c6165b93d11c/src/sys/sched.c#L910-L949) define transient boundaries. LUS [target handlers](https://github.com/Kenix3/libultraship/blob/759b13401cd6903daa67a00a753066f791622e78/src/fast/interpreter.cpp#L3115-L3121) retain addresses; [presentation](https://github.com/Kenix3/libultraship/blob/759b13401cd6903daa67a00a753066f791622e78/src/fast/Fast3dWindow.cpp#L232-L252) can return before command execution. **Falsifying experiment.** Acquire alternating-context live histories and both pass packages before overwrite, reproduce counts/focus from captured words, and compare instrumented versus uninstrumented publication. ## Runtime target: H5–H8

**Planner proposal.** RuntimePlanner proposed separate gameplay/service clocks, backend-neutral continuation admission, linked producer/mixer/device proof, acceptance/error ownership and typed sentinel/reset classes. Separate-stack contexts were preferred for migration size, not selected as an API.

**Reviewer challenge.** RuntimeReviewer identified the broad one-tick audio wording as the material contradiction; serialized native-thread handoff and complete suspension-transitive state machines are viable candidates, unlike concurrent actors/restarted callbacks. It emphasized original FP initialization, sink Null/drop behavior, command-level address narrowing and delayed completion across reset. Existing conditional contracts should be sharpened, not expanded into a new framework.

**Parent choice — amend H5/H7/H8, retain semantics and defer H6 backend.** One gameplay owner produces game-originated requests while measured retrace/sample-paced audio and input phases retain original ordering/coalescing. W04 owns cross-domain stall/pause traces and compares all three continuation mechanisms with FP, native stacks, zero waits, callback/self-end/return, deferred reclamation, cancellation/wakeup/join and compiler/ABI/supported-sanitizer evidence. W12 separately qualifies producer, mixer and sink; W07 owns truthful acceptance and exactly one terminal outcome for accepted work. Rejection retains ownership; drain-before-reuse or generation identity rejects stale completions. Wake before join and retain storage until accesses cease. Reset covers initialized overlay data and BSS, scene heaps, player-only/full-asset audio and persistent state. No API or mandatory telemetry feature is selected.

**Rejected alternative/tradeoff.** Forcing audio one-to-one with completed gameplay updates can change service progression **[INFERENCE]**; letting device callbacks advance gameplay is also rejected. Blanket `Acmd` widening or outer-pointer widening does not resolve narrowed inner operands. Occupancy/void-return success cannot prove acceptance. Immediate self-stack reclamation, unwoken joins and BSS-only reset can change lifetime/reset behavior **[INFERENCE]**. Explicit contracts cost evidence work, but a new scheduler/framework or broad portability inventory is not required.

**Source evidence.** [Independent audio retrace/bootstrap queues](https://github.com/ethteck/pokemonsnap/blob/df8815e75f3d79618466e01e0827c6165b93d11c/src/sys/audio.c#L741-L745), [audio quotas/completion](https://github.com/ethteck/pokemonsnap/blob/df8815e75f3d79618466e01e0827c6165b93d11c/src/sys/audio.c#L923-L989) and [gameplay draining](https://github.com/ethteck/pokemonsnap/blob/df8815e75f3d79618466e01e0827c6165b93d11c/src/sys/gtl.c#L699-L775) support distinct clocks. **Falsifying experiment.** Trace stall/pause retraces, gameplay updates, audio iterations/quotas and sound-state transitions; reduce clock distinctions only if measured equivalence supports it. Compare bounded nested-local/FP/callback/return/cancellation traces through continuation candidates on actual toolchain cells. Force unavailable/Null/full/failing sinks and account every generated frame through acceptance or explicit failure, then independently capture real output. Delay old completion across reset/reuse, close queues with waiters, mutate initialized overlay data and repeat player-only/full resets with outstanding users.

## Material gaps and unchanged boundaries

None is a demonstrated native implementation defect; no candidate's runtime sufficiency follows from source inspection. Rendering/backend/continuation APIs remain deferred until their discriminating evidence exists.

The faithful final scope, all courses/progression/photos/saves/original audio/ending/release requirements, W00–W15 identities, 34 closure edges and full W12-before-W08 boot/closure policy are unchanged. H1 changes only admission of accurately bounded nonplayable research, not whole-unit final acceptance. A passing small fixture proves only named coverage. Contracts and their future falsifiers are recorded here; no new runtime, extraction, permission or platform proof is claimed.
